# Check your shipment

Use your order confirmation and packing list for the contents of your purchase. A standard contents list for the V2 production package has not yet been confirmed.

Before installation, identify the board revision, inspect the headers and connectors, and check that each fitted driver is seated squarely. Photograph any shipping damage before applying power.

Do not assume the package includes a GIGA, flashed firmware, motors, external drivers, power supplies, cables or mounting hardware unless your order lists them. See [equipment required](prerequisites.md).
