## What's NOT Included

Based on the product information, the following items are **not** included and must be purchased separately:

- **Arduino Giga R1 WiFi**: The microcontroller board that the shield mounts onto
- **Stepper Motor Drivers**: Required to drive your motors (shield provides signals only)
- **Stepper Motors**: The actual motors for your motion control rig
- **RJ45 or DB37 Cables**: Cables to connect the shield to your motor drivers

## Packaging & Shipping

The manufacturer can ship DMC Lite Shields worldwide:

- **Stock Availability**: Due to low production volumes, limited stock is maintained. Contact the manufacturer to check current availability
- **Bulk Orders**: For orders of 5 or more units, manufacturing and worldwide shipping can be completed within 12 days
